# IFB air-conditioner Super Warranty — conditions summary

## Standard and extended periods

IFB describes a 12-month standard warranty for the original domestic purchaser, starting on the purchase date and covering qualifying defects in workmanship or materials, subject to exclusions. IFB then describes four additional years of Super Warranty on specified functional parts, giving the advertised five-year machine period when the product and customer meet the conditions. IFB separately describes nine additional years on the inverter compressor after the first year, giving ten years in total for qualifying compressor defects.

## Eligibility date and maintenance

IFB's terms state that the five-year warranty applies to products purchased on or after 15 May 2026 and units manufactured from January 2026. The Super Warranty remains valid only if the customer takes two paid annual preventive maintenance services each year from the second through the fifth year, booked with IFB and performed by its authorized service franchise. IFB offers two free preventive maintenance services during the first warranty year when booked through customer care; they cannot be carried forward beyond that first year.

## Parts and costs

The additional four-year Super Warranty names the outdoor and indoor PCBs, indoor and outdoor fan motors, swing motors, indoor and outdoor coils, and qualifying gas charging. IFB says replacement or repair is for manufacturing defects after authorized inspection. Additional expenses and visit charges are outside the Super Warranty. During the extended compressor warranty, gas charging and labour costs can be chargeable. IFB's separate gas-charging provision is narrower: up to five years, gas charging is covered when associated with replacement of a defective compressor, evaporator coil, or condenser coil, with applicable visit charges paid by the customer.

## Claim conditions and exclusions

IFB says unauthorized installation, service, repair, opening, or part modification can invalidate warranty claims. The terms ask for a completed warranty card and may require proof of ownership and purchase date, such as the sales invoice. The warranty is for the original purchaser and is not transferable. The terms exclude, among other things, damage from misuse or accidents, certain cosmetic or plastic/rubber issues, normal wear and tear, regular maintenance, and commercial or semi-commercial use. The full official terms control eligibility and exclusions.

Their star ratings describe energy performance, not a different warranty duration in those two IFB product listings. The eligibility and service conditions above still apply.
