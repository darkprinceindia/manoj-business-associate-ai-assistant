# IFB 5-star AC warranty reference — CI1852D223GN1

## Model and advertised warranty

IFB's product page for model CI1852D223GN1 advertises a five-year machine warranty, subject to conditions, and a ten-year compressor warranty. IFB lists this model as a 1.5-ton, 5-star Summer Series air conditioner. The 5-star label is the model's energy rating; it does not by itself establish different warranty cover from a 3-star model.

Official model page: https://www.ifbappliances.com/ci1852d223gn1

## Five-year cover eligibility

IFB's AC Super Warranty terms say the five-year warranty applies to a product purchased on or after 15 May 2026 and a unit manufactured from January 2026. A customer should check the sales invoice and unit details before assuming that this five-year cover applies to a particular purchase. IFB describes the five years as one standard year plus four years of Super Warranty, with conditions.

Official warranty terms: https://www.ifbappliances.com/ac-super-warranty-terms-and-conditions

## Compressor cover

The same IFB terms describe one standard warranty year plus nine additional years for the inverter compressor, for manufacturing defects after IFB inspection. During the extended compressor period, gas charging and labour costs can be charged to the customer. The product page advertises the total as ten years of compressor warranty.

Official warranty terms: https://www.ifbappliances.com/ac-super-warranty-terms-and-conditions

## Important conditions

IFB's terms require authorized installation and service. The four-year Super Warranty requires two paid annual preventive maintenance services each year from years two through five, booked through IFB and carried out by an authorized IFB franchise. Visit or other additional charges may apply. See the full IFB terms for exclusions and individual claim eligibility.

Official warranty terms: https://www.ifbappliances.com/ac-super-warranty-terms-and-conditions
